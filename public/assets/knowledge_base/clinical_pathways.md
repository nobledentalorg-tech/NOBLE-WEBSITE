# Noble Clinical Pathways: Treatment Options & Dilemmas

## Dilemma 1: Deep Tooth Decay (Touching Nerve)
**Patient Question:** "My tooth hurts and the decay is deep. What should I do?"
* **Context:** Bacteria have reached the pulp (nerve), causing Irreversible Pulpitis.
* **Option A: Root Canal Treatment (RCT)**
    * *Philosophy:* "Save the Natural Tooth."
    * *Procedure:* Clean the infected nerve, seal the root, and place a Crown.
    * *Benefit:* You keep your natural tooth and bone structure. Chewing feels normal.
    * *Risk:* 5-10% chance of re-infection over 10 years. Cost is higher than extraction.
* **Option B: Extraction (Removal)**
    * *Philosophy:* "Remove the Problem."
    * *Procedure:* Surgically remove the entire tooth.
    * *Benefit:* Instant pain relief. Lowest immediate cost.
    * *Risk:* Bone loss occurs immediately. Adjacent teeth will shift, ruining the bite. Replacing it later (Implant) costs 3x more than saving it now.
* **Clinical Guidance:** We prioritize Option A unless the tooth is structurally destroyed.

## Dilemma 2: Missing Tooth Replacement
**Patient Question:** "I lost a tooth. How do I fix it?"
* **Context:** A gap in the jawbone causes bone resorption.
* **Option A: Dental Implant (The Gold Standard)**
    * *What is it?* A titanium screw placed in the bone + a ceramic crown.
    * *Benefit:* Doesn't touch neighboring teeth. Lasts 20+ years. Preserves bone.
    * *Risk:* Requires minor surgery. Healing takes 3 months.
* **Option B: Dental Bridge**
    * *What is it?* A 3-unit cap that uses neighboring teeth for support.
    * *Benefit:* Fixed in 1 week. No surgery.
    * *Risk:* We must cut down two *healthy* teeth to support the fake one. Harder to clean underneath.
* **Clinical Guidance:** Option A is preferred for long-term health. Option B is preferred if the patient cannot undergo surgery (e.g., Uncontrolled Diabetes).

## Dilemma 3: Crooked Teeth (Alignment)
**Patient Question:** "I want to straighten my teeth."
* **Option A: Clear Aligners (Invisalign/ClearSteps)**
    * *Benefit:* Invisible, removable, easy to brush/floss.
    * *Limitation:* Patient compliance is key (must wear 22 hrs/day). Not for complex jaw surgeries.
* **Option B: Metal/Ceramic Braces**
    * *Benefit:* Works 100% of the time, even for complex rotations. Cheaper than aligners.
    * *Limitation:* Harder to clean. Visible. Can cause ulcers.

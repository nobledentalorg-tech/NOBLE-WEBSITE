Topic 1: Dental Anatomy
The tooth consists of enamel, dentin, and pulp. Enamel is the hardest substance in the human body, composed of 96% hydroxyapatite. Dentin forms the bulk of the tooth and is sensitive. The pulp contains nerves and blood vessels.

Topic 2: Physiology of Pain
Pain in the dental pulp is transmitted via A-delta fibers (sharp, fast pain) and C-fibers (dull, throbbing, slow pain). Inflammation causes increased pressure in the pulp chamber, leading to severe pain (Pulpitis).

Topic 3: Pathology
Caries is a biofilm-mediated disease. S. mutans is the primary initiator.

# Clinical Mechanisms: The Unified Chain

## 1. The Pain Pathway (Connecting Anatomy <-> Physio <-> Pharma)
* **Anatomical Structure:** Trigeminal Nerve (Mandibular Branch).
* **Physiological Process:** Nociception (Signal transmission via Sodium Channels).
* **Biochemical Trigger:** Tissue damage releases **Arachidonic Acid** -> Converted by **COX-2 Enzymes** -> Into **Prostaglandins** (Pain signals).
* **Pharmacological Block:**
    * **NSAIDs (Ketorolac):** Block **COX-2 Enzymes** (Stopping the Biochemistry).
    * **Lignocaine (Local Anesthesia):** Blocks **Sodium Channels** (Stopping the Physiology).

## 2. The Infection Pathway (Connecting Path <-> Micro <-> Pharma)
* **Pathological State:** Dentoalveolar Abscess (Pus accumulation).
* **Microbiology:** Anaerobic bacteria (Fusobacterium, Prevotella) multiply in low oxygen.
* **Biochemical Environment:** Acidic pH (makes Local Anesthesia less effective).
* **Pharmacological Action:**
    * **Amoxicillin:** Destroys bacterial cell wall (Peptidoglycan synthesis).
    * **Metronidazole:** Disrupts DNA of anaerobic bacteria.

## 3. The Healing Pathway (Connecting Systemic Physio <-> Surgery)
* **Physiological Process:** Clot formation (Fibrin mesh) -> Osteoblast migration (Bone healing).
* **Systemic Interruption (Diabetes):** High blood sugar (>200mg/dL) impairs neutrophil function (slow healing).
* **Systemic Interruption (Smoking):** Vasoconstriction acts as a barrier to oxygen supply (Dry Socket risk).
* **Clinical Implication:** Do not perform implants/extractions if HbA1c > 8.0 or Smoker.

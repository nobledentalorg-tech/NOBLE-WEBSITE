# Post-Operative Complications & Triage (The "Guardian")

## After Tooth Extraction (Removal)
* **Symptom:** Bleeding stops after 30 mins.
    * **Status:** Normal. Keep biting on gauze (cotton) for pressure.
* **Symptom:** Bleeding continues after 1 hour.
    * **Status:** Abnormal (Oozing).
    * **Action:** Place a wet tea bag on the socket (tannic acid stops bleeding). Call clinic.
* **Symptom:** Severe throbbing pain after 3 days.
    * **Status:** Dry Socket (Alveolar Osteitis). Clot has dislodged.
    * **Action:** Immediate clinic visit required for medicated dressing.

## After Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
* **Symptom:** Mild pain on biting/chewing.
    * **Status:** Normal for 2-3 days post-treatment. Inflammation settling.
    * **Action:** Take prescribed painkiller (NSAID).
* **Symptom:** Swelling in the cheek / Pimple on gum.
    * **Status:** Flare-up / Abscess.
    * **Action:** Antibiotics may need adjustment. Call clinic immediately.

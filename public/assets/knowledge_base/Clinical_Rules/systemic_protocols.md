# Systemic Disease Protocols (Whole Body Logic)

| Condition | Dental Risk | Pre-Op Requirement | Drug Contraindication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diabetes (Uncontrolled)** | Delayed healing, Dry socket (Alveolar Osteitis) | Check random blood sugar. If > 200 mg/dL, defer treatment. | Avoid Steroids (increases sugar). |
| **Hypertension (High BP)** | Excessive bleeding (Hemorrhage) | Check BP. If > 160/100, refer to physician. | Avoid Adrenaline in local anesthesia (use Plain Lignocaine). |
| **Pregnancy (1st Trimester)** | Fetal organ formation risks | Emergency pain relief only. No X-Rays. | Avoid NSAIDs (Ibuprofen). **Safe:** Paracetamol (Acetaminophen). |
| **Blood Thinners (Aspirin)** | Bleeding risk | Do NOT stop medication without cardio consult. Use local clotting agents (Hemostatic agents). | Avoid NSAIDs (increases bleeding). |

# Evidenced-Based Home Remedies

## 1. Clove Oil (Eugenol)
- **Use**: Temporary relief for Pulpitis/Toothache.
- **Mechanism**: Natural anesthetic (numbs nerve).
- **Limit**: Does NOT cure infection. Relief is temporary.

## 2. Warm Salt Water
- **Use**: Gum swelling / Post-extraction healing.
- **Mechanism**: Osmotic effect reduces edema (swelling).
- **Limit**: Supportive care only.

## 3. Cold Compress
- **Use**: Post-surgical swelling / Trauma.
- **Mechanism**: Vasoconstriction (reduces blood flow/swelling).
- **Limit**: Do NOT apply heat to an infection (spreads bacteria).

# Drug Interaction Safety Matrix

## 1. Anticoagulants (Blood Thinners)
- **Target**: Patients on Aspirin, Warfarin, Clopidogrel
- **Protocol**: Do NOT perform Extraction without physician clearance.
- **Risk**: Uncontrolled Bleeding.
- **Action**: Request INR value + Medical Consult.

## 2. Bisphosphonates
- **Target**: Patients on Osteoporosis medication
- **Protocol**: Avoid invasive surgery if possible.
- **Risk**: MRONJ (Osteonecrosis of Jaw).
- **Action**: Conservative management preferred.

## 3. Local Anesthesia + Hypertension
- **Target**: Patients with High BP (>160/100)
- **Protocol**: Limit Adrenaline containing LA.
- **Action**: Use plain Lignocaine or Mepivacaine.

# Noble Dental Drug Formulary (India/Hyderabad Market)

## Pain Management (Analgesics)
* **Mild Pain:** Paracetamol 650mg (Brand: Dolo 650).
    * **Safety:** Safe for pregnancy/gastritis (stomach ulcers).
* **Severe Dental Pain:** Ketorolac Tromethamine (Brand: Ketorol DT).
    * **Warning:** Do not take on empty stomach. Avoid if asthmatic (can trigger attack).
* **Nerve Pain:** Pregabalin + Methylcobalamin.
    * **Used for:** Trigeminal Neuralgia (Shooting Nerve Pain).

## Antibiotics (Infection Control)
* **Standard Infection:** Amoxicillin 500mg.
* **Severe Abscess (Swelling):** Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid 625mg (Brand: Augmentin 625 / Clavum 625).
    * **Side Effect:** Can cause diarrhea; take with probiotic (curd).
* **Penicillin Allergy:** Clindamycin 300mg.

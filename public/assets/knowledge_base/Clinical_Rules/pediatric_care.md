# Pediatric vs Adult Rules (Age Filters)

## Trauma: Avulsed Tooth (Knocked Out)
* **Patient Age < 6 (Primary/Milk Tooth):**
    * **Rule:** NEVER put it back in.
    * **Reason:** Risk of damaging the developing permanent tooth bud underneath.
    * **Action:** Control bleeding with gauze. Visit dentist for checkup.
* **Patient Age > 7 (Permanent/Adult Tooth):**
    * **Rule:** EMERGENCY. Replant immediately if clean.
    * **Action:** Pick by crown (white part), NOT the root. Wash gently. Store in cold milk/saliva. Rush to clinic within 60 mins.

## Toothpaste Guidelines (Fluoride Safety)
* **Age 0-3:** Rice-grain amount (Smear).
    * **Type:** Fluoride-free or Low Fluoride (500ppm).
* **Age 3-6:** Pea-sized amount.
    * **Rule:** Parent must supervise spitting. Do not swallow.
    * **Type:** Kids Fluoride Toothpaste (1000ppm).

# Clinical Protocols & Decision Trees

## Rule 1: Swelling + Fever
- **Symptom**: Swelling + Fever
- **Likely Cause**: Abscess (Spreading Infection)
- **Action**: Prescribe Amoxicillin + Metronidazole
- **Urgency**: High (Immediate Attention Required)
- **Referral**: Endodontist or Oral Surgeon

## Rule 2: Sharp Pain to Cold
- **Symptom**: Sharp zing lasting < 10 seconds
- **Likely Cause**: Reversible Pulpitis (Dentin Hypersensitivity)
- **Action**: Desensitizing Toothpaste + Evaluation
- **Urgency**: Moderate

## Rule 3: Lingering Pain to Hot
- **Symptom**: Dull throbbing ache lasting minutes/hours
- **Likely Cause**: Irreversible Pulpitis
- **Action**: Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
- **Urgency**: High

## Rule 4: Bleeding Gums
- **Symptom**: Blood while brushing
- **Likely Cause**: Gingivitis (Early Gum Disease)
- **Action**: Professional Scaling (Cleaning)
- **Urgency**: Low/Moderate

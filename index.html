    FAQs

    Common questions—clear answers

    Is teeth whitening safe?

    Yes when supervised. ADA guidance supports peroxide-based gels with proper gum protection; temporary sensitivity is common and resolves within 24–48h. See ADA.

    How long does an implant take?

    Typical timeline is 3–6 months from implant placement to final crown, depending on bone healing and whether grafting is needed. ITI/WHO emphasize smoking cessation and hygiene. See WHO and ITI consensus texts.

    Are X-rays safe?

    Dental radiographs use low doses; benefit outweighs risk when used judiciously with shielding. See FDA and ADA.

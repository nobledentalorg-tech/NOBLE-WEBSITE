FAQs

Common Questions — Clear Answers

Evidence‑based answers, patient friendly, referenced from NHS, ADA, and standard textbooks. Reviewed by Dr Dhivakaran & NDC‑D Team.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when supervised by a dentist. NHS & ADA guidance supports peroxide‑based gels with gum protection. Temporary sensitivity is common and usually resolves in 24–48h. NHS Reference.

How long does a dental implant take?

Typical healing is 3–6 months from implant placement to crown fitting, depending on bone quality and grafting needs. ITI Consensus.

Are dental X‑rays safe?

Dental radiographs use very low doses of radiation. Benefits outweigh risks when used appropriately with shielding. FDA Reference.

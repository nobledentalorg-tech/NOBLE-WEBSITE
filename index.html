FAQs

Common Questions — Clear Answers

How long does recovery take after a root canal?

Most patients experience mild discomfort for 1–3 days. Some sensitivity or swelling may last up to a week, while full tissue healing can take weeks. Source

How safe are dental X-rays?

Dental X-rays involve very low radiation, comparable to a few days of natural exposure. Protective shielding is used when required. NHS

How long does a root canal-treated tooth last?

With good oral hygiene and a crown restoration, root canal-treated teeth can last many years, often a lifetime. Source

Can I go back to work right after dental procedures?

In most cases, yes. Patients can return to normal activities the same day, but avoid chewing on treated teeth until numbness fades. Source

What should I do if I feel pain or swelling after treatment?

Mild swelling and pain are common, but if symptoms worsen after a few days, contact your dentist. Use prescribed medication, OTC pain relief, or cold compresses as directed. Source

Painless Root Canal

Endodontics Pain relief Tooth saving
Full details

A root canal removes infection inside the tooth while preserving the natural crown. According to the NHS, it has a success rate of 80–95% when followed by a protective crown.

Mild discomfort is normal for 2–3 days. Avoid hard chewing on the tooth until your crown is placed.

  • Use a soft-bristle toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Return for your crown within 1–2 weeks.
  • Report persistent pain/swelling to your dentist.
  • Pros: Saves natural tooth, avoids extraction, long-term function.
  • Cons: Crown required, not suitable if tooth is severely fractured.
  • NHS — Root Canal
  • ADA — Root Canal
  • Cohen’s Pathways of the Pulp, 11th ed. (Textbook)

FAQs

Common Questions — Clear Answers

Evidence-based answers, patient friendly, referenced from NHS, ADA, and standard textbooks. Reviewed by Dr Dhivakaran & NDC-D Team.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when supervised by a dentist. NHS & ADA guidance supports peroxide-based gels with gum protection. Temporary sensitivity is common and usually resolves in 24–48h. NHS Reference.

How long does a dental implant take?

Typical healing is 3–6 months from implant placement to crown fitting, depending on bone quality and grafting needs. ITI Consensus.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Dental radiographs use very low doses of radiation. Benefits outweigh risks when used appropriately with shielding. FDA Reference.

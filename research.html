Research & Innovation

At Noble Dental Care, we advance dentistry through clinical research, academic collaboration, and patient-centric evidence-based outcomes.

🧠 Ongoing Medical Studies

Our clinical studies focus on improving precision, safety, and longevity of treatments using microscopic and digital techniques.

  • Root Canal Precision Study

    Evaluating postoperative healing and long-term success using ZEISS Microscopic Endodontics.

    Design: Prospective Observational • Status: Active

  • Restorative & Aesthetic Outcomes

    Analyzing color stability and bonding integrity of composite restorations under Hyderabad humidity conditions.

    Design: Experimental • Status: Peer Review

  • Pediatric Behavior Response

    Comparing anxiety levels in children treated under laser vs. rotary pulp therapy.

    Design: Randomized Comparative • Status: Ethics Approved

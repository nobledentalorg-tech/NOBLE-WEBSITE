How does SHY-NM work?

  1. 1. CSPS meets saliva

    When SHY-NM comes into contact with saliva, the CSPS particles activate immediately.

  2. 2. pH elevation

    Sodium ions (Na+) are released, elevating the pH into the range essential for hydroxycarbonate apatite (HCA) formation.

  3. 3. Mineral release

    Calcium (Ca2+) and phosphate (P5+) ions are liberated from CSPS to create a protective calcium-phosphate layer.

  4. 4. HCA crystal formation

    The calcium-phosphate layer crystallises into HCA, sealing open dentinal tubules with a barrier that is chemically and structurally similar to natural enamel.

This HCA layer provides long-lasting protection against wear and pain, letting you enjoy food and beverages without sensitivity.

What is tooth sensitivity?

Tooth sensitivity is a pain or discomfort in one or more teeth that is triggered by hot, cold, sweet or sour foods and drinks or even by breathing cold air. The pain can be sharp, sudden and can shoot deep into the nerve endings of your teeth.

What exactly happens inside a sensitive tooth?

When the middle dentin layer is exposed, tiny openings called dentinal tubules allow stimuli to reach the nerve endings. Cold, heat or acidic triggers then cause sharp flashes of pain. SHY-NM seals these tubules so sensitivity does not return.

Use SHY-NM Toothpaste to get superfast relief from sensitivity while your dentist addresses the underlying cause.

Causes of tooth sensitivity

Tooth sensitivity causes fall into two major categories: damaged enamel or tooth surface, and receding gums.

Damaged enamel or tooth surface

  • Highly abrasive toothpaste.
  • Use of hard bristled toothbrush.
  • Forceful or horizontal tooth brushing.
  • Acidic foods and drinks.
  • Bruxism (teeth grinding).
  • Repeated vomiting (stomach acids).

Chemical dissolution from citrus fruits, juices and carbonated drinks is now a frequent reason for enamel loss.

Receding gums

  • Poor oral hygiene.
  • Inflammation and infection of the gums.
  • Inefficient tooth brushing technique.
  • Stress from improper bite alignment.

Managing tooth sensitivity

Do's

  • Consult your dentist immediately.
  • Use a desensitising toothpaste and mouthwash.
  • Brush twice daily with a soft bristled toothbrush.
  • Brush teeth with a proper tooth brushing technique.
  • Use a bite guard if you clench your teeth at night.
  • Maintain good oral hygiene.

Don'ts

  • Don’t self-medicate.
  • Don’t brush horizontally as it exposes the tooth root.
  • Avoid hot, cold, sweet or sour food that trigger pain.
  • Don’t over brush or use high abrasive toothpaste.
  • Don’t brush immediately after consuming acidic foods.
  • Don’t pick your teeth with metal objects.

Tooth sensitivity FAQs about SHY-NM Toothpaste

How fast will my tooth sensitivity reduce after using SHY-NM?

Most patients notice quick action within a couple of days of twice-daily brushing. For intense cold sensitivity, pair home use with an in-clinic desensitising varnish so you feel comfortable while the CSPS builds lasting protection.

Can I use SHY-NM daily along with fluoride toothpaste?

Yes. SHY-NM is fluoride-free and can be alternated with your prescription fluoride toothpaste. Use SHY-NM at night so the CSPS layer can form undisturbed while you sleep.

Is SHY-NM safe for children and pregnant women?

Children above 6 years can use a pea-sized amount under adult supervision. Expecting and nursing mothers can use it safely; please share your medical history with our dentists for a personalised brushing plan.

Do I still need a dental appointment if I use this toothpaste?

Yes. While SHY-NM controls tooth sensitivity at home, an in-clinic check identifies cracks, cavities or grinding issues that need treatment. Use this toothpaste as a protective layer between dental visits.

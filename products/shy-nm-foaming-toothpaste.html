Why patients with tooth sensitivity choose Shy NM

Therapeutic uses

  • Instant relief from cold, sweet and air-triggered tooth sensitivity.
  • Rebuilds a hydroxycarbonate apatite shield over exposed dentinal tubules.
  • Reduces biofilm growth and supports gum healing in periodontitis patients.
  • Protects restorative margins, implants and ceramic crowns from acidic wear.

Advantages for your smile

  • Foaming format reaches interdental and hard-to-brush surfaces quickly.
  • Fluoride-free, making it safe alongside in-office fluoride varnish.
  • Warm sensation indicates active ion exchange sealing the tooth surface.
  • Authored by Dr. Dhivakaran with medical review from Dr. Gana Prakash, MBBS & Dr. Rakesh, MBBS.

Who should use it

  • Adults and children above 6 years with generalised dentin hypersensitivity.
  • Patients post-scaling, whitening or periodontal therapy needing tubule closure.
  • Individuals with receding gums, cracked teeth or exposed root surfaces.
  • Aligner and brace wearers experiencing enamel demineralisation.

Key composition and science

CSPS (Calcium Sodium Phosphosilicate) reacts with saliva to raise the pH, release calcium and phosphate ions, and crystallise into a protective hydroxycarbonate apatite barrier that stops tooth sensitivity.

How to use Shy NM foaming toothpaste

1. Prep & dose

Apply a pea-sized amount onto a soft bristle brush for children under 6 and a ribbon for adults. Do not dilute with water to keep the CSPS concentration intact.

2. Target sensitivity zones

Brush for two minutes focusing on exposed root surfaces, along gum lines and sensitive molars. Allow the warm sensation to work for 30 seconds before rinsing lightly.

3. Seal & protect

Use twice daily for two weeks, then once daily for maintenance. Combine with in-clinic desensitising therapy every six months for long-term protection.

Tooth sensitivity FAQs about Shy NM Foaming Toothpaste

How fast will my tooth sensitivity reduce after using Shy NM?

Most patients experience a noticeable drop in sharp sensitivity within 5-7 days of twice-daily brushing. For intense cold sensitivity, pair home use with an in-clinic desensitising varnish for instant relief while the CSPS builds long-term protection.

Can I use Shy NM daily along with fluoride toothpaste?

Yes. Shy NM is fluoride-free and can be alternated with your prescription fluoride toothpaste. We recommend using Shy NM at night so the CSPS layer can form undisturbed while you sleep.

Is Shy NM safe for children and pregnant women?

Children above 6 years can use a pea-sized amount under adult supervision. Expecting and nursing mothers can use it safely; please share your medical history with our dentists for a personalised brushing plan.

Do I still need a dental appointment if I use this toothpaste?

Yes. While Shy NM controls tooth sensitivity at home, an in-clinic check identifies cracks, cavities or grinding issues that need treatment. Use this toothpaste as a protective layer between dental visits.

Controls plaque biofilm

How to brush with Amflor

Step-by-step routine

  • Use a soft orthodontic brush or electric brush head with a pea-sized amount of Amflor.
  • Angle bristles 45° to the gum line, brushing each bracket for 5 seconds.
  • Spit, do not rinse. Let the amine fluoride layer stay on teeth for at least 30 minutes.

Clinic monitoring

  • Monthly photo submissions keep track of white spot improvement.
  • Combine with Amflor Oral Rinse for braces with poor plaque scores.
  • Switch to SHY-NM or EnaFix if intense sensitivity persists.

Not suitable for patients with fluoride hypersensitivity. We customise alternatives for such cases.

Can I use Amflor with other fluoride products?

Yes. Use Amflor twice daily and space other fluoride gels or rinses at least one hour away to avoid dilution.

Does Amflor help with bad breath?

Regular use reduces plaque and improves breath freshness. Pair with Amflor Oral Rinse or probiotics for stubborn odour.

Is Amflor safe for long-term use?

Amflor is designed for continuous use throughout orthodontic therapy and beyond to maintain enamel resistance.

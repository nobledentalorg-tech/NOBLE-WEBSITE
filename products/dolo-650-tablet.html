Dosage timeline & safety checkpoints

Standard schedule

  • 1 tablet (650 mg) every 6 hours after food.
  • Maximum of 4 tablets in 24 hours.
  • Stop and call us if fever persists beyond 48 hours.

Who should avoid self-dosing

  • Patients with chronic liver disease or heavy alcohol intake.
  • Children under 12 years—require paediatric formulation.
  • Individuals already using other paracetamol combinations.

Can I combine Dolo 650 with ibuprofen?

Yes, staggered dosing with ibuprofen-based tablets like Ibugesic Plus is sometimes advised for severe pain. Always allow a 2-hour gap between medicines and follow the personalised plan we share.

What if I miss a dose?

Take the tablet as soon as you remember if at least 6 hours have passed since the last dose. Do not double up to compensate—call our dentist for adjustments.

How quickly will I receive the medicine?

Orders placed before 6 pm are delivered the same day within our service radius. We also keep a reserve pack ready for pick-up at the clinic reception.

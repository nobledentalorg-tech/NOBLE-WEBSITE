Directions to use EnaFix PROGEL

Clinic-approved steps

  • After brushing, apply the required amount onto your fingertip or tray.
  • Spread evenly over tooth surfaces with your finger or tongue.
  • Leave for at least 3 minutes, then spit out any excess. Do not rinse.
  • Avoid eating or drinking for 30 minutes after application.

Storage & follow-up

  • Store below 30°C in a dry place. Do not freeze.
  • Keep the cap tightly closed to maintain freshness.
  • Weekly photo reviews help us monitor remineralisation progress.

Ideal for patients with dry mouth, acid reflux, post-orthodontic white spots and early enamel lesions needing a gentle nightly boost.

Can kids use EnaFix PROGEL?

Yes, children above 6 years can use the gel under supervision. Apply a smaller amount and ensure they spit out the excess.

How often should I apply the gel?

Most patients use it nightly for 4-6 weeks. Our dentist may increase or decrease frequency based on enamel response.

Can I combine it with fluoride toothpaste?

Absolutely. Brush with fluoride toothpaste, spit, avoid rinsing and then layer EnaFix PROGEL for deeper mineral absorption.

    Checkout FAQs

    What happens after I submit the cart?

    Your email client and WhatsApp open with a pre-filled message so nothing is missed. Send both and our care team starts preparing your order.

    Will the ₹50 delivery fee change for multiple products?

    No. The ₹50 covers a single doorstep drop anywhere in Nallagandla 500019 regardless of the number of items in your cart.

    How are the 25% and 10% discounts applied?

    The cart shows your expected savings. During billing our clinic software applies 25% to dental products and 10% to antibiotics and pain relief lines linked to your order ID.

    What if I need to add a prescription?

    Upload it inside the product drawer before checkout or reply to the WhatsApp message with a clear photo. Our clinicians verify it before dispatch.

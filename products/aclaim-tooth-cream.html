How we prescribe Aclaim

Nightly application

  • Brush normally, spit but do not rinse. Dry teeth with gauze or tissue.
  • Apply a pea-sized amount of Aclaim with a clean finger or tray, spreading across white spot areas.
  • Leave undisturbed for at least 3 minutes, then spit out the excess. Avoid food or drink for 30 minutes.

Follow-up checkpoints

  • Week 1: Dentist reviews sensitivity and salivary flow via tele-consult.
  • Week 4: In-clinic fluorescence scan to measure mineral gain.
  • Week 8: Decide on continuation, ICON infiltration or fluoride varnish.

Patients with milk protein allergy should avoid CPP-ACP creams. We offer alternative fluoride or bioactive glass protocols on request.

Can I use Aclaim with fluoride toothpaste?

Yes. Continue your regular fluoride toothpaste twice daily and apply Aclaim after brushing at night. Spit, do not rinse, before applying the cream.

Is Aclaim safe for children?

Children above 6 years can use Aclaim with adult supervision to avoid swallowing. Younger children should use dentist-guided alternatives.

How long before I see results?

Most orthodontic patients notice softer white spots and reduced sensitivity within 4-6 weeks. Persistent lesions may need ICON or fluoride varnish.

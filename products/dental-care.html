Dental kit guidance

Frequently asked questions about home dental care

Our clinicians ensure the right protocol for your enamel and dentin while you use these kits at home.

How often should I use Shy NM or GC Tooth Mousse?

Shy NM can be used twice daily in place of regular paste. GC Tooth Mousse is typically applied nightly for 7-10 days or as guided by our dentist.

Can kids use these products?

Enafix is fluoride-free and safe for kids under supervision. For other products, check with our pedodontist via the free tele consultation.

Do I still need an in-clinic visit?

These kits stabilise symptoms but in-clinic evaluations confirm root causes. Bring your order ID to avail the 25% consultation discount.

Usage schedule & lifestyle tips

Daily routine

  • Dissolve one sachet in 50 ml cool water after evening brushing.
  • Sip slowly and avoid food or drink for 30 minutes.
  • Continue for 30 days, then reassess with our team.

Enhance benefits

  • Pair with chlorhexidine rinses or antibiotics at least 2 hours apart.
  • Limit sugary snacks and increase leafy greens to feed healthy bacteria.
  • Store sachets in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

Our nutrition team tracks your response via WhatsApp check-ins and can adjust dosage for children or seniors.

Can children use these probiotics?

Yes, children above 6 years can use a half sachet daily under dentist supervision. We customise the plan during consultation.

How soon will I notice benefits?

Most patients report fresher breath within 5 days and improved gum comfort within 2 weeks. Consistency is key.

Can I continue during antibiotic therapy?

Absolutely—just separate doses by at least 2 hours to preserve probiotic efficacy. Our team guides exact timings.

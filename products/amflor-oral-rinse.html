How we direct Amflor rinse

Usage schedule

  • Use 10 ml undiluted twice daily after brushing with Amflor toothpaste.
  • Swish for 60 seconds, focusing on brackets and gumline.
  • Do not rinse with water afterwards; avoid food or drink for 30 minutes.

Clinic follow-up

  • Weekly hygiene check-ins help us track white spot reduction.
  • Alternate with chlorhexidine during flare-ups of gingivitis if advised.
  • Combine with interproximal brushes for best results.

Not recommended for children below 6 years. Supervise teens to ensure they spit the rinse and do not swallow.

Should I dilute the rinse?

No. Use Amflor Oral Rinse undiluted to maintain its fluoride strength.

Can I use it along with chlorhexidine?

Alternate the rinses at least one hour apart. We will tailor the schedule if you need both.

Does it stain teeth?

Amflor is alcohol-free and does not stain. Maintain brushing routine to keep brackets glossy.

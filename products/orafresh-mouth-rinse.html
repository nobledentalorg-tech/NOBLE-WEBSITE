Usage pattern & stain prevention

How to rinse

  • Measure 10 ml undiluted and swish for 30 seconds.
  • Avoid eating or drinking for 30 minutes after rinsing.
  • Use morning and night for 7 days, then pause for 7 days unless advised otherwise.

Avoid these mistakes

  • Do not mix with toothpaste—rinse 30 minutes after brushing.
  • Limit coffee, tea and wine to reduce temporary staining.
  • Do not swallow. Keep out of reach of children below 6 years.

We customise rinse duration for pregnant patients, orthodontic wearers and post-surgical cases to avoid overuse.

Will OraFresh stain my teeth?

Mild brown stains can appear with prolonged use. We limit courses to 7 days and provide polishing if stains appear.

Can children use this rinse?

Only children above 12 years should use OraFresh, and under adult supervision. Younger children may swallow the rinse accidentally.

Can I use OraFresh long-term?

No. Long-term chlorhexidine use can disturb taste and oral microbiome. We alternate with probiotics and gentle mouthwashes for maintenance.

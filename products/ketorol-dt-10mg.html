Dosage & safety guardrails

How to take Ketorol DT

  • Dissolve 1 tablet under the tongue or in 10 ml water when pain peaks.
  • Allow at least 8 hours between doses. Do not exceed 3 tablets in 24 hours.
  • Combine with Dolo 650 only after dentist confirmation.

Contraindications

  • Active gastric ulcers, GERD or bleeding disorders.
  • Chronic kidney disease, pregnancy or lactation.
  • Concurrent NSAID or anticoagulant therapy without medical clearance.

Each prescription includes a telephonic follow-up within 6 hours to monitor pain scale, stomach comfort and need for escalation to in-clinic care.

Can I drive after taking Ketorol DT?

Ketorol DT may cause dizziness in some patients. Avoid driving or operating machinery until you know how your body responds.

Is Ketorol DT safe after dental surgery?

Yes, when used for a short course under supervision. We often prescribe it after complex extractions with a protective stomach regimen.

What if stomach irritation starts?

Stop the medicine and contact us immediately. We will shift you to an alternative analgesic and arrange a physician review.

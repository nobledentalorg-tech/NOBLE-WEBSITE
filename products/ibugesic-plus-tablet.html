Dosage pattern & NSAID precautions

How to take Ibugesic Plus

  • 1 tablet every 8 hours after a full meal.
  • Maximum 3 tablets per day for up to 3 consecutive days.
  • Hydrate well and avoid additional NSAIDs during the course.

Safety checklist

  • Avoid if you have gastric ulcers, asthma triggered by NSAIDs, or kidney concerns.
  • Not advised during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
  • Inform us about blood thinners or antihypertensive medication before starting.

We log your medication start and end dates in our digital records and coordinate with your physician to prevent duplicate NSAID exposure.

Can I take Ibugesic Plus with antibiotics?

Yes, it can be combined with dentist-prescribed antibiotics like Augmentin or Metrogyl. Share your medication list so we can schedule doses safely.

What if stomach irritation develops?

Take the tablet strictly after meals and inform us immediately. We will adjust the plan or add a protective antacid if needed.

How soon should I see results?

Most patients notice pain reduction within 45 minutes and improved swelling within 24 hours. If discomfort escalates, call us for escalation protocols.

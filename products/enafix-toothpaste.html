Why preventive patients choose Enafix

  • Rehardens enamel softened by acid reflux, xerostomia or acidic diets.
  • Improves the appearance of fluorosed and hypomineralised teeth.
  • Supports orthodontic patients experiencing demineralisation around brackets.

Advantages for your smile

  • Fluoride-free formulation safe alongside in-office fluoride varnishes.

Who should use it

  • Patients with frequent snacking, high sugar intake or orthodontic appliances.

  • Children needing a fluoride-free alternative for daily cavity prevention.
  • Adults noticing white spot lesions or early enamel chalkiness.

Key composition and science

The Anticay 5% complex (Calcium Sucrose Phosphate + Inorganic amorphous calcium phosphate) breaks down in saliva to release calcium and phosphate ions, reducing enamel solubility and initiating remineralisation.

How to use Enafix Tooth Paste

Morning routine

  • Apply a pea-sized amount onto a soft bristle toothbrush.
  • Brush for two minutes covering all tooth surfaces and gum margins.
  • Expectorate without rinsing aggressively to allow mineral deposition.

Night routine

  • Repeat brushing before bedtime for sustained ion release while you sleep.
  • For children below 6 years, supervise and use only a peanut-sized amount.
  • Pair with interdental cleaning to limit plaque retention between teeth.

Clinic guidance

  • Use exactly as advised by Noble Dental Care dentists for 4-6 weeks.
  • Schedule review visits to monitor white spot regression and enamel lustre.
  • Discontinue and consult if sensitivity, irritation or allergic response occurs.

Enafix Tooth Paste FAQs

Is Enafix Tooth Paste suitable for all age groups?

Yes. Enafix can be used by children and adults as a fluoride-free alternative. Use only a peanut-sized amount for children under 6 years and supervise brushing.

How long does Enafix take to strengthen enamel?

Visible remineralisation is typically observed within 2-4 weeks of twice-daily use. Continue maintenance brushing once daily after the initial phase.

Can Enafix be combined with fluoride varnish therapy?

Yes. Since Enafix is fluoride-free, it complements in-clinic fluoride varnishes. Follow your dentist's instructions for scheduling each therapy.

Does Enafix cause any side effects?

No side effects are commonly reported. If you notice irritation or allergic reactions, discontinue use and contact Noble Dental Care immediately.

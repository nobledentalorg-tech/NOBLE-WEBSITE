Speciality FAQs

Answers to the most common questions we receive before patients begin treatment.

Can I combine multiple speciality treatments in one visit?

Yes. We coordinate orthodontic, periodontal, implant or restorative appointments on the same day when clinically safe, supported by on-site imaging and anaesthesiology.

Are your treatments pregnancy-safe?

Pregnancy dental care visits include trimester-based risk screening, lead-shielded imaging and liaison with your gynaecologist before any intervention.

Do you provide EMI or flexible payments?

Transparent treatment plans include 0% EMI partners, phased payments for aligners/implants and digital receipts after every visit.

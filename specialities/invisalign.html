Corrections aligners handle beautifully

Stage-by-stage expansion, controlled stripping and root uprighting open space without extractions in most mild-moderate cases.

Overbite & deep bite

Know the limitations before you commit

Clear aligners are powerful yet not universal. We keep you informed of biomechanical boundaries so expectations stay realistic.

Skeletal discrepancies

Severe class II or III jaw relationships usually need braces, growth modulation or surgery. Aligners alone cannot reposition bone bases.

Impacted or ankylosed teeth

Teeth stuck in bone require surgical exposure and fixed mechanics for traction before finishing with aligners.

Compliance sensitivity

20–22 hour wear is non-negotiable. Lost trays or inconsistent wear extends timelines and may need mid-course correction.

Severe rotations & open bites

Rotations over 35° or open bites exceeding 4 mm often respond better to braces or hybrid plans with TAD support.

If aligners cannot meet goals alone, we propose hybrid pathways blending short-term braces, TADs or sectional appliances before resuming trays.

Do's and don'ts during Invisaligner treatment

Consistency protects your investment. Follow these habits to keep trays crystal clear and treatment on schedule.

Do this daily

  • Wear trays 20–22 hours and switch on schedule.
  • Rinse aligners with lukewarm water and brush gently every morning and night.
  • Seat trays using chewies to ensure full engagement.
  • Log wear time in our compliance app for AI reminders.

Weekly rituals

  • Soak trays in retainer cleaning crystals.
  • Photograph your smile for Dr. Deepak's review.
  • Floss nightly and use fluoride rinse to guard enamel.

Avoid these mistakes

  • No hot water or harsh cleaners — they warp trays.
  • Do not eat or drink anything except water with aligners in place.
  • Skipping attachments or elastics delays correction.
  • Leaving trays in napkins invites accidental disposal.

Aligner FAQs we answer daily

Transparent answers support EEAT best practices and help you make a confident decision.

How long will my Invisaligner treatment take?

Express cases finish within 3–6 months. Comprehensive cases average 12–18 months with scheduled refinements if mid-course adjustments are needed.

Will I need attachments or elastics?

Most comprehensive plans use tooth-coloured attachments for grip. Elastics are prescribed for bite correction and we provide templates plus reminders inside our app.

Are refinements chargeable?

Two refinement rounds are included in Noble Aligners. Additional refinements require only material costs as diagnostics stay valid for 12 months.

What happens after treatment?

We provide removable retainers, optional bonded retainers and yearly reviews. Retention is crucial — expect night wear for at least 12 months, then alternate nights.

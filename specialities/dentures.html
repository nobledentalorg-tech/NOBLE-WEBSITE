What happens at each step

We translate our lab's fabrication protocols into chairside actions so you know exactly how your denture is engineered.

Digital planning

  • Records: iTero scan, CBCT (if implants), phonetic video.
  • Lab ticket: Secure portal with tooth mould, shade, occlusal scheme.
  • Preview: On-screen 3D design with patient approval.

Try-in refinement

  • Evaluate lip support, smile arc, fricative sounds and midline.
  • Mark sore spots with PIP paste and adjust wax/printed base.
  • Confirm VDO using Willis gauge and digital articulation.

Delivery & education

  • Border moulding polish, occlusal equilibration and liner application.
  • Chewing ladder with soft-to-firm food progression.
  • Printed maintenance plan with immersion, cleaning and recall dates.

Questions we answer every week

Still unsure? These clarifications come straight from our denture consults.

How do I choose between Acrylic Plus, Inject, BPS, Nova and PEEK options?

Acrylic Plus works beautifully for first-time wearers needing dependable suction. Acrylic Inject and Inject Pro step up durability with pressure injection and balanced articulation. BPS adds full instrumentation and Ivocap processing for premium precision, while Nova and Flex lines cushion sensitive ridges. CAD/CAM PEEK or DMLS upgrades are best when you want implant-ready strength and long-term adaptability.

Can I upgrade to implant-supported dentures later?

Absolutely. Every denture is recorded with digital scans so we can add locator housings later. CAD/CAM PEEK and DentCare DMLS prostheses already include relief for attachments, and when you're ready we place implants, capture pick-up impressions and convert the denture into a titanium-supported overdenture.

What maintenance is required after delivery?

Follow the cleaning ladder we provide: rinse after meals, soak nightly in non-abrasive cleanser and schedule annual relines. Your plan includes a complimentary liner replacement within 6 months plus cloud-stored records so remakes are effortless.

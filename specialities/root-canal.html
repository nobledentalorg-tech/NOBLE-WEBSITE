Signs you may need a root canal in Hyderabad

Emergency toothache, lingering sensitivity or deep decay are warning signals. We triage quickly so you do not have to wait in pain.

Spontaneous or night pain

Sharp, throbbing pain that wakes you up is the pulp asking for help. Analgesics stop working quickly.

Hot & cold linger

Temperature sensitivity lasting longer than 30 seconds means irreversible pulpitis that needs an endodontic rescue.

Swelling or sinus tract

Pus draining near the tooth, puffy gums or facial swelling signal infection spreading beyond the tooth.

Fractured tooth structure

Deep cracks, trauma or failed fillings expose the pulp. Microscope inspection helps decide between RCT or onlay.

Single-visit root canal workflow at Noble Dental

Hover or tap the timeline to see what happens during your appointment. Most molars are completed in one sitting; complex cases may need a second visit.

Step 1 · Comfort setup

Computer-controlled anaesthesia (The Wand) and isolation with rubber dam. Mild sedation available on request.

Step 2 · Access & location

Microscope-guided access conserves enamel. Ultrasonics locate hidden MB2, C-shaped or accessory canals safely.

Step 3 · Rotary shaping

Nickel-titanium rotary files with apex locator guidance sculpt canals accurately while maintaining natural anatomy.

Step 4 · Activated disinfection

Sodium hypochlorite, EDTA and chlorhexidine activated via ultrasonic + Er:YAG laser pulses remove biofilm.

Step 5 · Bioceramic seal

Warm vertical compaction with bioceramic sealer seals micro-spaces. Post-obturation CBCT verifies healing path.

Step 6 · Same-day crown

CAD/CAM scan, design and mill. Chairside zirconia or e.max crown bonded before you leave for full protection.

Aftercare: Soft diet for 24 hours, analgesics only if required, 48-hour virtual review, in-clinic check in 7 days and recall X-ray at 6 months.

Root canal FAQ for patients & caregivers

Answers align with AAE 2024 guidelines and our internal clinical governance board.

Will the root canal hurt?

With digital anaesthesia and laser-activated disinfection the procedure feels like a long filling. You may experience mild biting sensitivity for 24-48 hours which resolves with prescribed medication.

Can I get a root canal and crown in one visit?

Yes. Our CAD/CAM workflow lets us scan, design and bond a zirconia or e.max crown in about 45 minutes after obturation so the tooth is protected immediately.

How do you ensure sterilisation?

We use Class B autoclaves, single-use rotary files for high-risk canals, cold chain irrigants and ATP monitoring to validate sterilisation every cycle.

Do you treat children or special needs patients?

Yes, in collaboration with our pediatric and special care dentistry team. We offer nitrous oxide, protective stabilisation and hospital dentistry referrals if deeper sedation is required.

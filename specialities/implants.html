🦷 Types of Dental Implants

Based on bone quality, missing teeth, and desired speed — we design the most suitable system for you.

Single Tooth Implant

Used when one tooth is missing — an implant replaces its root and a ceramic crown restores function & aesthetics.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Two or more implants hold multiple crowns — avoiding the need to trim natural teeth. Ideal for larger gaps.

Full-Arch (All-on-4 / All-on-6)

Four to six implants support a full arch of fixed teeth. Perfect for complete tooth loss or denture replacement.

🧬 PRF & Bone Grafting – Healing Science

Harnessing your body’s natural biology and biomaterials for safe, predictable implant success.

🩸

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF)

PRF uses your own blood to create a fibrin membrane rich in growth factors, accelerating healing and reducing swelling.

  • 100% autologous – no chemicals or additives
  • Promotes soft-tissue closure
  • Enhances graft stability and osseointegration
🦴

Autograft – Your Own Bone

Bone harvested from your jaw or chin region — the gold standard for biological compatibility and integration speed.

  • Best biological match
  • Rapid vascularization
  • Ideal for sinus lifts or small defects
🌿

Xenograft – Bio-Derived Graft

Processed bovine mineral scaffold that integrates gradually as your bone grows — safe and clinically tested.

  • Slow resorption supports bone volume
  • Excellent for aesthetic zones
  • High clinical success (20+ years evidence)
⚗️

Alloplast – Synthetic Bone

Made from calcium phosphate ceramics — ideal when patient’s donor bone is limited or minimal defect correction is needed.

  • Fully biocompatible
  • Available in granules or putty form
  • Provides predictable scaffold for new bone

🩺 After-Care & Recovery Guide

Precision-driven healing plan — follow these clinical do’s ✅ and don’ts ❌ for optimum osseointegration.

✅ Recommended Actions

  • Maintain soft diet (soups, yogurt, smoothies)
  • Apply cold compress on cheek (10-15 min cycles)
  • Rinse gently after 24 hrs using prescribed mouthwash
  • Complete your prescribed antibiotics and painkillers
  • Use ultra-soft brush near implant site
  • Sleep with head elevated first night

❌ Avoid These

  • Touching surgical site with fingers or tongue
  • Forceful spitting or rinsing for 24 hrs
  • Smoking or alcohol consumption (min 1 week)
  • Hard or chewy food (nuts, meat, chips) for 7 days
  • Heavy workouts or bending for 48 hrs

🧩 Frequently Asked Questions

Answers reviewed by Dr. Dhivakaran & implantology team, aligned with ADA and ITI global protocols.

💉 Is a dental implant painful?

No — implants are placed under local anaesthesia or conscious sedation. Mild soreness may occur for 1–2 days, easily managed with prescribed analgesics.

Source: Mayo Clinic, 2024
🦷 How long does a dental implant last?

With proper oral hygiene and professional review, implant success exceeds 95% at 10 years and can last lifetime.

Source: Clin Oral Implants Res, 2021
⏱️ When can I resume work after surgery?

Most patients return to work the next day. Only avoid strenuous activity for 48 hours to minimise swelling.

Source: ADA Guidelines
🩸 Can diabetics or heart patients get implants?

Yes — provided systemic condition is stable and physician-cleared. Controlled diabetes and cardiac patients show outcomes comparable to healthy individuals.

Source: J Clin Periodontol, ITI Consensus 2022
🦴 What if I don’t have enough bone?

Bone grafting or sinus lift procedures can regenerate volume. Guided implants with PRF enhance healing.

Source: Int J Oral Maxillofac Surg, 2022
💰 What is the cost of a dental implant?

Prices in Hyderabad range ₹25K–₹60K depending on brand, bone quality, and prosthesis. Includes CBCT, PRF, and lifetime warranty on implant body.

Source: Noble Dental Analytics, 2025 Regional Cost Survey

Medical Evaluation We Do (Simple & Safe)

We coordinate with your physician where needed. Safety first, always.

Diabetes

HbA1c
  • We request your recent HbA1c; typically aim ≤ 7–7.5%.
  • Antibiotic and healing protocols tailored to your risk.
  • Uncontrolled diabetes → stabilise first.

Hypertension & Cardiac

BP
  • We check BP at visits; local anaesthetic adjusted as needed.
  • Recent MI/stroke (≤ 6 months) → defer & seek clearance.

Bleeding & Medications

INR
  • Anticoagulants/antiplatelets: physician plan + local haemostasis.
  • High-dose IV antiresorptives: discuss alternatives.

Smoking & Oral Health

Quit
  • Pause smoking 1–2 weeks pre-op & 4–8 weeks post-op.
  • Gum health & plaque control strongly influence success.
Professional guidance: We follow ITI, ADA/AAOMS and national bodies (ICMR). See sources.

The Implant Journey — Step by Step

  1. 1) Consult, Photos & CBCT

    We capture your bite, smile line and bone data. You’ll preview a digital plan.

  2. 2) Digital Planning

    We virtually position implants away from sinus/nerve. Guides improve precision.

  3. 3) Gentle Placement (Local Anaesthesia)

  4. 4) PRF Support (When Indicated)

    Platelet-rich fibrin supports soft-tissue healing and graft stability.

  5. 5) Healing (8–12 weeks typical)

    Bone integrates with the implant. We review and maintain gum health.

  6. 6) Abutment & Digital Impression

    Connector fitted; we scan for a custom crown/bridge.

PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) — Your Biology, Your Boost

What it is

A small blood draw is spun to concentrate platelets and growth factors into a fibrin mesh placed around the surgical site.

Why we use it

May improve soft-tissue healing and graft handling. It’s autologous (from you), reducing reaction risks.

When

Common in grafts, sinus lifts, immediate implants and thin soft-tissue biotypes.

Bone Grafting & Sinus Lift — When We Need More Bone

Minor Grafts

  • Particulate or block graft to thicken ridge walls.
  • Often alongside implant placement.

Sinus Lift (Lateral/Internal)

  • Raises sinus floor to gain height for upper back implants.
  • Use PRF/membranes for stability.

Materials

  • Autograft (your bone), allograft, xenograft, alloplast.
  • We select based on site & evidence.

Follow-Ups & Longevity

Immediate

  • Day 0–2: cold compress, soft diet, routine pain relief.
  • No smoking or vigorous rinsing for 48–72 hours.

Short-term

  • Sutures at 7–14 days.
  • Hygiene review; tailor brushes/floss aids.

Long-term

  • Reviews at 3–4 months (integration), then 6–12 months.
  • With good control, implants often last decades.

Lifestyle Guidance — Diet, Hygiene & Activity

Diet & Habits

First 48–72h: soft/cool foods; avoid hot/spicy and alcohol. No straws (negative pressure). Gradually resume normal diet per advice.

Oral Hygiene

Gentle brushing day 1 with soft brush; avoid the surgical site for 24h. Use prescribed rinse from day 1–5. Interdental aids after review.

Activity

Avoid heavy exercise for 48–72h. Sleep with head elevated first night. No smoking or vaping during early healing.

Longevity & Survival Rates

With healthy gums, good hygiene and risk control (e.g., diabetes, smoking), many implants last decades. Literature commonly reports 5-year survival ~95%+ and 10-year ~90%+ depending on site and risk profile (see sources).

Non-promise note: Outcomes vary with bone quality, bite forces, systemic health and maintenance. We build your plan around these factors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is “teeth in a day” always possible?

Only when insertion torque and bone quality permit. Otherwise, we allow routine healing (8–12 weeks) for predictable results.

Are zirconia implants better than titanium?

Titanium remains the most studied and versatile. Zirconia can be considered for metal-sensitive patients or specific esthetic demands; case selection is key.

What if I grind my teeth (bruxism)?

We often recommend a night guard, occlusal adjustments, and periodic screw checks to protect your work.

Lifestyle & Care After Implants

Diet

  • Soft diet for 1–2 weeks (soups, dal, rice, yogurt).
  • Avoid hard/brittle foods (nuts, popcorn) until crown placement.
  • Hydrate well; avoid alcohol and carbonated drinks early post-op.

Oral Hygiene

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled brush.
  • Use interdental brushes or water flossers for implant sites.
  • Chlorhexidine rinses may be advised during early healing.

Exercise & Recovery

  • Light walking/yoga can resume in 48 hours.
  • Avoid heavy lifting or high-impact sports for 1–2 weeks.
  • Always use a mouthguard if resuming contact sports.

Longevity & Success Rates of Dental Implants

Modern implants have excellent long-term outcomes when maintained with reviews and hygiene.

  • 10-year survival rates: 94–97% (ITI Consensus).
  • 20-year survival: Many still functional, especially in non-smokers with good hygiene.
  • Risk factors lowering longevity: uncontrolled diabetes, smoking, poor oral hygiene, peri-implantitis.
  • With routine care and follow-up, implants can last decades, often lifelong.
Source: ITI Consensus 2018, ADA Guidelines, Misch’s Contemporary Implant Dentistry.

The 6-stage Noble Smile Design journey

From the first conversation to long-term retention, the process is transparent, co-created and documented so you know what happens at every milestone.

Vision Consultation & Facial Study

We record HD photos, videos, intraoral scans and a short interview about how you want to feel when you smile. Our designers map facial thirds, lip mobility and tooth display at rest and while speaking.

  • Digital portrait series and smile emotions capture.
  • Phonetic recordings to analyse speech consonants.
  • Smile goals worksheet curated with your priorities.

Digital Smile Blueprint

Using DSD & 3Shape Smile Design, we design new tooth proportions and gingival harmony that respect your facial archetype. You receive a cinematic simulation and 3D printable mock-up files.

  • Golden proportion & RED analysis overlay.
  • Video walkthrough of proposed smile movements.
  • Cloud shared plan for feedback with loved ones.

Mock-up & Trial Smile Day

We print the design and transfer it onto your teeth without drilling. You feel the new contours, speak, laugh and take photos to confirm the look. Adjustments happen chairside.

  • 3D printed shells placed with flowable composite.
  • Feedback from our cosmetic dentist, periodontist and you.
  • Confidence check: speech, laugh line, selfies.

Minimally Invasive Preparation

Only after you approve the trial do we prepare teeth. Guided silicone indexes and depth cutters limit reduction to 0.3–0.5 mm for veneers, preserving enamel and pulp vitality.

  • Rubber dam isolation and microscope magnification.
  • Pain-free with topical gels and The Wand® anesthesia.
  • Same-day provisionals mirror the final design.

Lab Artistry & Final Seating

Our partner ceramists craft lithium disilicate or hybrid ceramic veneers with layered translucency. We check contacts, occlusion and phonetics before cementing, then stage a reveal shoot.

  • Photorealistic shading and surface texture artistry.
  • Blue-light curing with heat and pressure control.
  • Reveal photoshoot & video for your keepsake.

Long-term Smile Maintenance

You leave with retainers, at-home care kits, dietary counselling and a maintenance calendar. Reviews at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months ensure the smile ages beautifully.

  • Personalised follow-up schedule and reminders.
  • Enamel strengthening serums & desensitising gels.
  • Complimentary retouch polishing at annual visit.

Smile design FAQs

Answers to the most common questions asked in our consultation lounge.

Will veneers damage or weaken my natural teeth?

No. We stay within enamel wherever possible and use depth-limiting burs and silicone guides. Adhesive cementation to enamel actually reinforces tooth strength and is reversible for repairs.

How long does the full smile design journey take?

Most makeovers complete within 14–21 days: consultation and scans (Day 1), design review (Day 5), mock-up (Day 7), preparation (Day 10) and final seating (Day 14). Destination patients can choose an accelerated 7-day protocol.

What if I need to spread payments?

We partner with leading finance providers for 0% EMI up to 12 months. Corporate and wedding packages include phased invoicing aligned with treatment stages.

Can you combine orthodontics with smile design?

Yes. Mild crowding or spacing can be corrected with in-house aligners before veneers. For complex cases, our orthodontist sequences braces or aligner therapy first and we plan veneers as the finishing layer.

Will my teeth be shaved a lot for veneers?

We design minimal-prep veneers guided by 3D reduction keys. For many cases we stay within enamel, which keeps the tooth strong and allows for future revisions if needed.

Can I test the smile before it becomes permanent?

Yes. The trial smile stage lets you wear a temporary mock-up for 48 hours. We adjust length, width and shade based on your feedback before crafting the final restorations.

How long do veneers and bonding last?

With night guard use and hygiene reviews, ceramic veneers last 12–15 years on average. Composite bonding may need polishing or refresh every 3–5 years depending on habits.

What if I grind my teeth at night?

We measure bite forces and provide a custom guard. If grinding is severe we may combine Botox for muscles or adjust bite contacts so your smile remains protected.

Do you treat outstation or NRI patients?

We offer virtual consults, itinerary planning, airport transfers and condensed 5-day smile makeovers for patients visiting Hyderabad.

Ready to script your smile reveal?

Share a selfie or book a video consult. Our smile concierge will schedule your design session, send pre-visit prep and reserve lab slots so timelines stay on track.

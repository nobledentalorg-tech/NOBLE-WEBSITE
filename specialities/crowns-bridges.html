Why crowns and bridges are essential after tooth damage

Leaving a tooth fractured or root canal treated without a definitive crown often leads to vertical cracks, reinfection and shifting bite. Our structured workflow protects the tooth, balances occlusion and preserves facial harmony.

Structural reinforcement

  • Encases weakened tooth walls after root canal or large fillings
  • Distributes biting load across the full crown circumference
  • Prevents microleakage and recurrent decay under restorations

Occlusal stability

  • Maintains vertical dimension, preventing TMJ strain
  • Bridges stop drifting of adjacent teeth into missing spaces
  • Protects opposing enamel with polished, contoured surfaces

Esthetics & phonetics

  • Shade-matched ceramics restore natural light reflection
  • Supports lip posture and speech by recreating tooth anatomy
  • Corrects spacing and proportion as part of smile design

Bridge configurations engineered for longevity

Custom CAD-CAM workflows allow precision pontics, hygienic emergence profiles and screw-retained implant bridges when teeth are missing.

Conventional tooth-supported bridges

  • Monolithic or layered zirconia up to 4 units with reinforced connectors
  • Margin design respects periodontal biotype to avoid recession
  • Ideal when abutment teeth have healthy root length and parallelism

Implant-supported full-arch

  • Hybrid PEEK or titanium frameworks with nano-ceramic or zirconia overlay
  • Screw-retained for retrievability and hygiene maintenance
  • Immediate load option with progressive torque protocol

Adhesive & minimal prep bridges

  • Maryland-style zirconia wings for young patients or anterior spacing
  • High-bond resin cements paired with enamel preservation
  • Best for single missing incisors with stable occlusion

Do’s ✅ and Don’ts ❌ for long-lasting crowns

Follow these guidelines during the first 72 hours and throughout the life of your crown or bridge to maintain the glaze and cement seal.

✅ Do this

  • Use non-abrasive toothpaste and soft brush around margins
  • Floss with crown-safe threaders or water flosser nightly
  • Wear prescribed night guard if you clench or grind
  • Schedule bite check 7–10 days after cementation
  • Rinse with alcohol-free mouthwash to protect resin cement

❌ Avoid this

  • Crushing ice, nuts or hard candies during first week
  • Using teeth to open packets or bottles
  • Skipping professional cleaning — plaque weakens margins
  • Chewing tobacco or acidic gummies that stain ceramic
  • Delaying review if sensitivity or rocking is felt

Clinical checkpoints

  • Annual X-ray ensures margins remain sealed
  • Glaze refresh every 24 months for nano-ceramic hybrids
  • Torque check for implant bridges every 6 months

Tooth conditions that modify crown or bridge selection

Before finalising a crown, we evaluate residual tooth structure, periodontal support, occlusion and systemic health to avoid premature failure.

Limited crown height

  • Choose Solid Plus zirconia or DMLS PFM to maximise retention at 0.8 mm thickness.
  • Plan crown lengthening if ferrule height drops below 2 mm circumferentially.
  • Avoid printed hybrids unless bonded to additional prep grooves.

High caries or acidic saliva

  • Use Classic/Ultra Plus zirconia or Nova PFM with glass ionomer bases for seal.
  • Book quarterly fluoride varnish; avoid Permaprint hybrids without glaze maintenance.
  • Monitor diet to limit pH swings that break down adhesive cements.

Parafunction / bruxism

  • Prefer BruxCare zirconia, Solid Plus or high noble alloy to absorb load.
  • Night guard mandatory when using layered zirconia or printed hybrids.
  • Design bridges with 12 mm² connectors and splint adjacent units.

Gum recession & thin biotype

  • Platinum/Premium layered zirconia masks metal-free while staying lifelike.
  • Avoid metal collars; select supragingival margins and pink porcelain if needed.
  • Polish emergence profile to protect delicate papilla.

Minimal inter-arch space

  • Select Solid Plus zirconia or high noble alloy at 0.8 mm clearance.
  • PEEK hybrids only when ≥4 mm vertical room allows composite layering.
  • Digitally equilibrate occlusion prior to milling or casting.

Compromised abutment / post core

  • Use DMLS or Nova PFM for customised post-and-core grip and solder-free strength.
  • Upgrade to implant-supported bridge if root fracture risk is high.
  • Maintain >2 mm ferrule and evaluate with CBCT when doubt remains.

Bond-dependent enamel

  • Reserve IPS e.max or Lava Ultimate for preparations with ≥70% enamel for bonding.
  • Sandblast and silanate before adhesive cementation to maximise bond strength.
  • Switch to zirconia or metal if dentin exposure dominates and isolation is compromised.

Metal sensitivity & soft tissue care

  • Choose PEEK hybrids or high-translucency zirconia when nickel sensitivity is reported.
  • Layer pink composite or ceramic to mask recession in thin biotype cases.
  • Limit non-precious alloys to posterior zones with impeccable hygiene compliance.

Precision workflow from scan to seat

Track how your chosen crown progresses through the studio — from digital impression to surface glazing — so you know exactly what to expect at each visit.

01

Intraoral scan & smile design

Photogrammetry, face scan integration and shade mapping capture every contour for multi-layer zirconia, e.max or nano-ceramic cases.

Deliverables: digital wax-up, prep guides, minimal reduction maps.
02

Material prescription

We match tooth position, occlusal space and parafunctional risk to the right material — from Ultra Plus to PEEK or high noble alloy.

Data points: flexural requirement, translucency grade, cement strategy.
03

CAD nesting & milling

Five-axis CAM milling or 3D printing produces frameworks with micron-checked margins before layering, pressing or infiltration.

Tech: multi-layer zirconia discs, lithium disilicate press furnaces, nano-ceramic polishers.
04

Surface artistry & QA

Layered ceramics, stain-and-glaze cycles and laser sintering go through loupe inspection, digital microscope verification and shade confirmation.

Checklist: proximal contact mapping, margin photos, glaze integrity.
05

Seat, adjust & protect

Clinical seating includes occlusal equilibration, bite scan verification, adhesive or luting protocol and custom night guard where needed.

Follow-up: 72-hour review, 6-month hygiene, annual radiographic check.

Pick the right restorative matrix for every tooth and patient

We don’t believe in a one-material-fits-all approach. Every filling is a hybrid decision based on remaining dentine, saliva control, occlusal load, age and caries risk.

Composite (Ivoclar)

Layered nano-hybrid composite

Ideal for load-bearing premolars & molars when we can isolate perfectly. High polish, stain resistance and cusp-by-cusp build-up for a natural bite.

  • Tetric Prime & Tetric PowerFill dentin + enamel shades
  • Micro-brush tinting to recreate fissure depth
  • Heat-hardened for higher flexural strength
GIC (GC)

High fluoride glass ionomer

Moisture-tolerant, fluoride releasing layer for root caries, cervical lesions and geriatric mouths where remineralisation is critical.

  • GC Fuji IX GP Extra bulk for cervical coverage
  • Sustained fluoride recharge from GC MI Paste support
  • Gentle on exposed root dentine, reduces sensitivity
Sandwich technique

Bioactive sandwich restorations

GIC base plus composite overlay to secure both chemical bond and occlusal strength — our go-to for deep class II & V lesions.

  • GC Fuji II LC liner sealed with Ivoclar Tetric EvoCeram
  • SonicFill adaptation for tighter proximal contacts
  • Bioactive liners like Biodentine/TheraCal when pulp is close
Deep caries management

Selective caries excavation

We respect the pulp. Atraumatic rotary instruments, caries detector dyes and stepwise excavation protocols to avoid unnecessary root canals.

  • CBCT + DIAGNOdent to map lesion depth pre-operatively
  • Calcium silicate liners (TheraCal, Biodentine) over near exposures
  • Re-entry reviews at 8-12 weeks when stepwise excavation indicated
Pediatric smiles

Hall-free kiddie fillings

Tell-show-do, wand anaesthesia and pre-appointment virtual familiarisation to keep baby teeth calm while we restore them.

  • Riva Star + Fuji II LC for fluoride-rich primary molars
  • Cartoon guided breathing + nitrous for anxious kids
  • Caries-risk recall every 3 months with SMART sealants
Permanent teeth

Occlusion-mapped permanent restorations

Digital bite scan overlays to ensure we respect existing contacts and create self-cleansing grooves that prevent new decay.

  • T-Scan® + articulating paper for shimstock validated contacts
  • Onlay planning when cuspal coverage is safer long-term
  • Polishing to 0.1 μm for plaque-resistant surfaces

A restorative workflow engineered for longevity & comfort

Every filling is choreographed to mimic nature — from caries detection to occlusion verification and patient after-care.

  1. 01 Digital diagnostics — iTero scan, bite analysis & cavity risk charting
  2. 02 Anaesthesia without fear — The Wand STA + pre-cooling and vibe distraction
  3. 03 Clean field — rubber dam isolation, retraction cords or OptraGate to block saliva
  4. 04 Material choreography — adhesives warmed to 39°C, incremental curing, glycerine oxygen barrier
  5. 05 Occlusion sculpt — 3D matrices, occlusal stamps, T-Scan verification & final glaze
  6. 06 Seal & protect — fluoride/CPP-ACP recharge, hygiene instructions & recall reminders

FAQ: everything about fillings at Noble

Quick answers to the questions patients and referring dentists ask us most.

How long do composite fillings last?

Our Ivoclar composites, placed with rubber dam isolation and heat-assisted curing, routinely last 7–12 years. With nightguards for grinders and 6-month hygiene, many cross the 15-year mark.

Will a deep cavity always need a root canal?

Not if we intervene correctly. Selective caries removal, calcium silicate liners and staged excavation allow the pulp to heal. We monitor vitality and sensitivity at every review.

Are GIC fillings only for kids?

No. Modern GC glass ionomers deliver high fluoride release and chemical bonding. We use them for elderly patients with recession, for ART in field camps and as bases under composite.

What about aesthetics on front teeth?

We layer multiple enamel and dentin shades, add tints for mamelons and finish with mylar to achieve a seamless surface that matches your natural fluorescence.

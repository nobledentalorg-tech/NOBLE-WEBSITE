Prescription safety card

Verified by Dr. Gana Prakash & Dr. Rakesh

Augmentin 625 DUO Tablet (Amoxicillin 500 mg + Clavulanic Acid 125 mg)

Therapeutic indication
Dental abscesses, ENT, respiratory, urinary, skin/soft tissue and bone infections susceptible to amoxyclav.
Dosage prescribed
Typically 1 tablet every 12 hours with meals for 5-7 days. Follow your physician’s exact schedule.
How to take
Swallow whole at the start of a substantial meal to improve absorption and minimise gastric upset. Maintain adequate hydration and balanced meals throughout therapy.
Common side effects
Diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting, thrush or mild skin rash. Report severe symptoms immediately.
Contraindications
History of cholestatic jaundice or hepatic dysfunction with amoxyclav, severe renal impairment without dose adjustment.
Allergic warning signs
Hives, facial or throat swelling, breathing difficulty, wheezing, dizziness or extensive rash require emergency care.

Always complete the prescribed course. Adequate food intake is essential to tolerate antibiotics and reduce gastric irritation.

Understand how Augmentin works

Clinical uses of Augmentin 625 DUO

Dental & ENT infections

  • Dental abscesses, periodontal infections and post-procedural prophylaxis.
  • Acute otitis media, sinusitis and tonsillitis with susceptible bacteria.
  • Skin, soft tissue and bone infections requiring broad coverage.

How it works

  • Amoxicillin inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis causing lysis.
  • Clavulanic acid deactivates beta-lactamase enzymes preventing resistance.
  • Combination extends spectrum against gram-positive and gram-negative organisms.
  • Authored by Dr. Dhivakaran with medical review from Dr. Gana Prakash, MBBS & Dr. Rakesh, MBBS.

Who requires caution

  • Patients with liver disease, renal impairment or previous jaundice on amoxicillin.
  • Individuals on warfarin, methotrexate, allopurinol or mycophenolate mofetil.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women (use only if clearly necessary).
  • Children under 12 years may require alternate dosage forms.

Safety checklist before dispensing

Contraindications

  • History of allergy to penicillin, amoxicillin, clavulanate or any beta-lactam antibiotic.
  • Previous antibiotic-associated liver dysfunction or jaundice.
  • Uncontrolled renal impairment without adjusted dosing.

Common side effects

  • Diarrhoea, nausea or vomiting.
  • Oral or vaginal thrush due to altered microbiome.
  • Skin rash or itching—requires immediate medical review.

Monitoring advice

  • Complete prescribed course; do not stop early even if symptoms improve.
  • Maintain hydration and take with meals to reduce gastrointestinal discomfort.
  • Report severe diarrhoea, jaundice, dark urine or allergic symptoms immediately.

Augmentin 625 DUO FAQs

What infections can Augmentin 625 DUO treat?

It is prescribed for dental infections, sinusitis, otitis media, urinary tract infections, skin and soft tissue infections, septic abortion and abdominal infections caused by susceptible bacteria.

How should I take the tablet?

Swallow the tablet whole with water at the times prescribed. Take with food to minimise gastrointestinal upset. Never exceed the prescribed dose.

What if I miss a dose?

Take it as soon as you remember, but leave at least 4 hours before the next scheduled dose. Do not double dose to compensate.

When should I contact the clinic urgently?

Contact us immediately if you experience jaundice, severe diarrhoea, persistent vomiting, difficulty breathing, facial swelling or skin rash.
